PAC-MAN
=======

Start:     ./pac-man config.json      (keep the _internal folder next to it)

Controls:  Arrows / WASD   move
           P / Esc         pause (Resume / Main Menu)
           Enter           select in menus

Rules:     Eat every pacgum to clear the level (10 levels).
           Big pacgums in the corners make ghosts edible for 7 seconds.
           Touching a normal ghost costs a life. Time up also costs a life.

Cheats:    C toggles cheat mode, then:
           I invincible, F freeze ghosts, V speed, L extra life, N skip level

Config:    Edit config.json (lives, points, seed, level time, maze sizes).
Scores:    Top 10 are saved in highscores.json.
